## 1. FinOps Principles

- Teams need to collaborate.
- Decisions are driven by business value of cloud.
- Everyone takes ownership for their cloud usage.
- FinOps data should be accessible and timely.
- A centralized team drives FinOps.
- Take advantage of the variable cost model of the cloud.

## 2. Cost Benchmarking

- Establish KPIs for cloud spend.
- Compare costs against industry standards and internal historical data.
- Track unit economics (e.g., cost per transaction, cost per user).

## 3. Rightsizing

- Analyze resource utilization (CPU, memory, network).
- Downsize or terminate underutilized resources.
- Use auto-scaling to match capacity with demand.
- Leverage spot instances for fault-tolerant workloads.
- Purchase Reserved Instances or Savings Plans for predictable workloads.

## 4. Tools

- Cloud-native tools: AWS Cost Explorer, GCP Billing, Azure Cost Management.
- Third-party tools: Cloudability, CloudHealth, Kubecost (for Kubernetes).
