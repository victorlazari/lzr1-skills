# Advanced French Linguistics (C1-C2)

This reference covers advanced topics suitable for C1 and C2 learners, focusing on literature, pragmatics, and sociolinguistics.

## Literature and Stylistics
- **Passé Simple:** Used almost exclusively in formal writing and literature. C1/C2 learners must recognize it and understand its function in narrative pacing.
- **Subjonctif Imparfait/Plus-que-parfait:** Highly literary tenses. Recognition is required for reading classic literature.
- **Stylistic Inversion:** Used in formal writing after certain adverbs (e.g., *Peut-être est-il parti*).
- **Figures of Speech:** Metaphor, metonymy, synecdoche, litotes, and hyperbole.

## Pragmatics and Sociolinguistics
- **Register (Le Registre de Langue):**
  - *Soutenu* (Formal/Literary)
  - *Courant* (Standard)
  - *Familier* (Informal/Colloquial)
  - *Argot* (Slang)
- **Verlan:** A type of French slang involving the inversion of syllables in a word (e.g., *femme* -> *meuf*).
- **Regional Variations:** Differences in vocabulary and pronunciation across Francophone regions (e.g., Quebec, Senegal, Belgium, Switzerland).
- **Politeness Strategies:** Advanced use of the conditional for politeness, nuanced use of *tu* vs. *vous*.
