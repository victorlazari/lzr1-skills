# French Grammar Curriculum (A1-C2)

This document outlines the standard progression of French grammar topics across the CEFR levels.

## A1 (Beginner)
- Present tense of regular -er, -ir, -re verbs
- Common irregular verbs (être, avoir, aller, faire)
- Definite and indefinite articles
- Gender and number of nouns and adjectives
- Basic negation (ne... pas)
- Possessive adjectives
- Asking simple questions (est-ce que, inversion)

## A2 (Elementary)
- Passé composé with avoir and être
- Imparfait (basic usage for descriptions)
- Futur proche (aller + infinitive)
- Direct and indirect object pronouns (le, la, les, lui, leur)
- Reflexive verbs
- Comparative and superlative of adjectives
- Relative pronouns (qui, que)

## B1 (Intermediate)
- Passé composé vs. Imparfait (nuanced usage)
- Plus-que-parfait
- Futur simple
- Conditionnel présent
- Subjonctif présent (basic triggers: il faut que, je veux que)
- Pronouns y and en
- Relative pronouns (dont, où)

## B2 (Upper Intermediate)
- Conditionnel passé
- Subjonctif présent (advanced triggers, expressions of doubt/emotion)
- Passive voice
- Gerund and present participle
- Reported speech (concordance des temps)
- Complex relative pronouns (lequel, auquel, duquel)
- Logical connectors (cependant, néanmoins, bien que)

## C1 (Advanced)
- Subjonctif passé
- Passé simple (recognition in literature)
- Nuances of hypothetical clauses (si clauses)
- Advanced negation (ne... que, ne... point)
- Stylistic inversion
- Nominalization

## C2 (Mastery)
- Passé antérieur (recognition)
- Subjonctif imparfait and plus-que-parfait (literary)
- Mastery of all stylistic devices and registers
- Idiomatic expressions and colloquialisms
- Deep understanding of pragmatics and sociolinguistics
