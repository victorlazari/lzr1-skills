# Dialectal Variations Reference

## ASALE Regional Classifications
This section covers regional variations of Spanish aligned with the Asociación de Academias de la Lengua Española (ASALE) classifications.

- **Peninsular Spanish:** Features of Spanish spoken in Spain (e.g., use of 'vosotros', 'ceceo'/'seseo').
- **Latin American Spanish:** Broad features common across Latin America (e.g., 'seseo', use of 'ustedes' for plural 'you').
- **Specific Regions:** Nuances of Mexican, Rioplatense (use of 'voseo'), Andean, and Caribbean Spanish.

## Cultural Sensitivity Audits
Guidelines for ensuring cultural sensitivity when teaching regional variations.

- **Slang and Idioms:** Be aware of regional slang that may be offensive in other regions (e.g., 'coger' in Spain vs. Latin America).
- **Contextual Usage:** Teach the appropriate context for regional terms to avoid misunderstandings.
