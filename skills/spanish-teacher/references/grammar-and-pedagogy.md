# Grammar and Pedagogy Reference

## Grammar Rules (RAE 2025)
This section outlines key grammar rules aligned with the Real Academia Española (RAE) "Nueva gramática de la lengua española" (2025 edition).

- **Nouns and Gender:** Standard rules for masculine and feminine nouns.
- **Verbs:** Conjugation patterns for regular and irregular verbs across all tenses and moods (indicative, subjunctive, imperative).
- **Pronouns:** Usage of subject, object, and reflexive pronouns.
- **Prepositions:** Common prepositions and their usage.

## Inclusive Language Guidelines
Guidelines for using inclusive language in Spanish, respecting RAE recommendations while acknowledging evolving usage.

- **Gender-Neutral Endings:** Use of '-e' or '-x' in informal contexts, while adhering to standard masculine plurals for mixed groups in formal contexts as per RAE.
- **Alternative Phrasing:** Strategies for avoiding gendered language by using collective nouns or rephrasing sentences.

## Pedagogical Strategies
Effective strategies for teaching and learning Spanish.

- **Comprehensible Input:** Providing language input that is slightly above the learner's current level (i+1).
- **Spaced Repetition Systems (SRS):** Using tools like Anki for vocabulary retention.
- **Communicative Language Teaching (CLT):** Focusing on interaction and meaningful communication.
