# Curriculum Schemas Reference

## CEFR and ACTFL 2024 Mapping
This section maps curriculum progression schemas to both CEFR (A1-C2) and ACTFL 2024 proficiency levels.

| CEFR Level | ACTFL Level | Description | Key Grammar Topics |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Novice Low/Mid | Beginner | Present tense (regular), basic vocabulary, greetings. |
| A2 | Novice High/Intermediate Low | Elementary | Preterite vs. Imperfect, basic future, daily routines. |
| B1 | Intermediate Mid/High | Intermediate | Subjunctive (present), conditional, expressing opinions. |
| B2 | Advanced Low/Mid | Upper Intermediate | Past subjunctive, complex sentences, abstract topics. |
| C1 | Advanced High/Superior | Advanced | Nuanced grammar, idiomatic expressions, professional contexts. |
| C2 | Distinguished | Mastery | Near-native fluency, complex literary and academic texts. |
