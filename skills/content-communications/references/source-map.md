# Content & Communications Source Map

This source map replaces the passive reading list and dictates when to consult specific authoritative guidelines.

**Verified against upstream: 2026-08-07**

## 1. Technical Writing & Style

Consult these sources when establishing editorial standards, writing technical documentation, or resolving style disputes.

- **Google Developer Documentation Style Guide**
  - **URL**: https://developers.google.com/style
  - **Use for**: General technical writing style, tone, formatting, and word choice.
- **Microsoft Writing Style Guide**
  - **URL**: https://docs.microsoft.com/style-guide
  - **Use for**: Software UI text, accessibility guidelines, and inclusive language.
- **Technical Writing Standards and Style Guidelines (USU)**
  - **URL**: https://engineering.usu.edu/students/ewc/writing-resources/technical-writing-standards
  - **Use for**: Academic and formal technical writing standards.

## 2. Accessibility

Consult these sources when creating digital content to ensure it is accessible to all users.

- **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1**
  - **URL**: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/
  - **Use for**: Normative requirements for digital accessibility (Levels A and AA).

## 3. Corporate Communications & PR

Consult these sources when planning corporate announcements, crisis communications, or PR strategies.

- **10 Corporate Communications Best Practices For B2B Success In 2025**
  - **URL**: https://www.fame.so/post/corporate-communications-best-practices
  - **Use for**: Modern B2B corporate communication strategies and multi-channel approaches.

## 4. Copywriting & Marketing

Consult these sources when crafting marketing copy, landing pages, or email campaigns.

- **A Guide to Effective and Persuasive Copywriting in Marketing**
  - **URL**: https://www.park.edu/blog/a-guide-to-effective-and-persuasive-copywriting-in-marketing/
  - **Use for**: Persuasive writing techniques and marketing copywriting fundamentals.
- **Copywriting Guidelines (UNA)**
  - **URL**: https://www.una.edu/digital-standards/copywriting/
  - **Use for**: Institutional and brand-aligned copywriting standards.
